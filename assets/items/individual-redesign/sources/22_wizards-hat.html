⚙️ How Wizard's Hat Works

Wizard's Hat is the Tome equivalent of Pot (Stainless Steel). While Pot now only affects weapon level caps, Wizard's Hat specifically targets Tome (passive book) level caps.

Effect Details

  • +10 Tome Level Cap from the first Wizard's Hat
  • Each additional copy grants one fewer level until reaching a +2 minimum
  • The ninth and later copies continue to grant +2 Tome levels each
  • Does NOT affect weapon level caps

💡 Pro Tip

Use both items together for maximum scaling! Pot handles weapon levels while Wizard's Hat handles tome levels.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does Wizard's Hat do?
The first Wizard's Hat raises every Tome level cap by +10. Later copies grant one fewer level per copy until the bonus reaches a +2 minimum.
When was Wizard's Hat added?
It was added in v1.0.49 (Christmas Patch, December 25, 2025) as a companion item to the reworked Pot (Stainless Steel).
Does Wizard's Hat affect weapons?
No. Wizard's Hat only affects Tomes. Use Pot (Stainless Steel) for weapon level caps.
Does Wizard's Hat have diminishing returns?
Yes. The first copy grants +10 Tome levels, then each copy grants one fewer until the bonus reaches +2; later copies remain at +2.
How do I unlock Wizard's Hat?
Wizard's Hat is a Legendary item available in the normal item pool; it has no separate challenge unlock requirement.
