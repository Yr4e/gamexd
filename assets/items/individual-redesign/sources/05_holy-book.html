🔓 How to Unlock Holy Book

Defeat the first Stage Boss without taking any damage. If you’re struggling, practice a short route to the boss, clear space, then start the fight with full resources.

  • Community proof-of-concept: multiple “no-hit Holy Book unlock” runs on video.
  • Try safe-control weapons and keep distance to avoid chip damage.

🧪 How the Healing Aura Works

Holy Book’s numbers are straightforward (+100 Max HP, +50 HP Regen). The unique part is the healing-triggered damage aura—each time you gain HP, nearby enemies take damage. This turns sustain into offense, especially in clustered fights.

Design note

The aura’s strength comes from how often you heal, not from attack speed. Builds that provide frequent small heals get the most uptime.

🔗 Smart Synergies & Play Ideas

These are recommended play ideas based on the aura’s trigger. They aren’t developer-stated rules, but they feel great in practice:

Steady sustain (e.g., Medkit)

Frequent regen ticks repeatedly trigger the aura. Pair with safe kiting to burn waves passively.

Healing zones (Campfire)

Stand in a regen aura to keep Holy Book pulsing during boss and elite packs.

Defensive weapons (Aegis)

Control space while your heals convert into AoE damage.

Tanky characters (Athena)

High HP + regen makes full use of Holy Book’s stats and aura uptime.
