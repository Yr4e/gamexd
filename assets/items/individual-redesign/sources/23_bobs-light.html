Bob's Light Item Guide

Epic Item

Quick answer: Bob's Light is an Epic item that releases a light explosion every 45 seconds. It kills nearby non-boss enemies; bosses take 4% Max HP damage instead. Unlock it by making lamp-charging damage deal the finishing blow to Big Bob on Graveyard.

How to Unlock Bob's Light

  1. Reach Big Bob on Graveyard. Complete the four-key Crypt route and enter the boss arena.
  2. Fight normally at first. The current requirement does not demand lamp-only damage for the entire fight.
  3. Stop uncontrolled damage near the end. When Big Bob is low, move away, stop firing toward him, and avoid damage-triggering items.
  4. Charge a lamp and let its damage finish Big Bob. The lamp damage must deliver the killing blow.
  5. Claim the unlock. Check the Unlocks menu after the run and purchase it there if the game presents a Silver purchase step.
Current rule: only the finishing blow needs to come from lamp-charging damage. Older guides that require every point of boss damage to come from lamps are outdated.

How Bob's Light Works

The pulse is centered on your character. It does not require a weapon hit and is most valuable once enemy density becomes high enough that ordinary weapons struggle to clear the screen.

Why the Bob's Light Unlock Did Not Register

A weapon got the last hit

Stop attacking earlier and face manual-aimed projectiles away from Big Bob.

An item proc stole the kill

Disable or avoid poison, Thorns, explosions, orbiting damage, and other autonomous damage sources for the final phase.

You stood near the lamp but did not charge it

Confirm the lamp is active and that its damage is visibly ticking before waiting for the finishing blow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unlock Bob's Light?

Kill Big Bob on Graveyard with damage from charging a lamp as the finishing blow.

Do all attacks against Big Bob need to come from lamps?

No. Under the current rule, only the finishing blow must come from lamp-charging damage.

Does Bob's Light instantly kill bosses?

No. Bosses take 4% of their Max HP from a pulse instead.

Does Bob's Light work on Red Ghosts?

Yes. Each pulse has a 33% chance to instantly kill a Red Ghost; otherwise it deals 4% Max HP damage.

Is Bob's Light the same as Bob (Dead)?

No. Bob's Light is a periodic centered explosion, while Bob (Dead) is a separate movement-triggered item.
