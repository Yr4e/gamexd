🧪 How Turbo Skates Works

Turbo Skates turns flat Movement Speed into Attack Speed, letting speed-focused builds reach new DPS ceilings. The conversion is capped at +40% Attack Speed per stack, so multiple copies scale cleanly without breaking the cap per stack.

✓ Advantages

  • Directly monetizes every source of Move Speed
  • Pairs naturally with sprinting/evade-heavy play
  • Lets ranged and melee builds alike hit higher APS tiers

✗ Disadvantages

  • No defensive value on its own
  • Requires external Move Speed sources to shine

🔓 How to Unlock Turbo Skates

Unlock Requirement

  1. In a single run, level the Cooldown Tome to Lv.5.
  2. In the same run, level the Agility Tome to Lv.5.
  3. Once both reach Lv.5, Turbo Skates is permanently unlocked and added to your item pool.
💡
Tip: Pick early Move Speed boosters so the item becomes impactful immediately after it drops.

🔗 Best Synergies & Playstyle

Core Pairings

Turbo Socks

Flat Move Speed that directly feeds Turbo Skates’ conversion. A staple pairing for speed-centric builds.

Cowards Cloak (inferred)

Boosts Move Speed on hit; with Skates this effectively yields extra Attack Speed during combat spikes.

Weapons that love more Attack Speed

Lightning Staff

Higher APS increases proc frequency and stun chaining potential.

Katana

Melee clears feel noticeably snappier as swing cadence ramps up.

Revolver

Attack Speed ups rate of fire; great for kiting & hit-and-run play.

❓ FAQ

📌
Is Turbo Skates Epic or Rare? It is currently listed as Epic on the community Megabonk Wiki (updated 2025-10-27). Some older pages list it as Rare.
What’s the exact cap?
+40% Attack Speed per stack (conversion cap).
Best way to use it?
Stack Move Speed (e.g., Turbo Socks) and keep moving; the item passively turns speed into DPS.
