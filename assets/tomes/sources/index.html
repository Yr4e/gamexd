Default Tomes

These Tomes are available from the start of every run. No unlock required!

Agility Tome

Movement Speed | +15%

Increases movement speed, improving positioning, kiting ability, and map traversal.

Unlock: Available from start

Cooldown Tome

Attack Speed | +7.5%

Reduces attack cooldowns, dramatically increasing DPS output and screen coverage.

Unlock: Available from start

Damage Tome

Damage Multiplier | +0.08×

Increases base damage for all attacks. The foundation of every offensive build.

Unlock: Available from start

Evasion Tome

Evasion | +10%

Grants a chance to completely avoid incoming damage through dodging.

Unlock: Available from start

Golden Tome

Gold Gain | +12%

Increases gold dropped from enemies and chests, enabling more shop purchases.

Unlock: Available from start

Health Tome

Max HP | +25

Increases maximum health, providing a larger buffer against burst damage.

Unlock: Available from start

Knockback Tome

Knockback | +20%

Increases knockback on attacks, pushing enemies away and providing space.

Unlock: Available from start

Precision Tome

Critical Hit | +2%

Increases critical hit chance, enabling devastating burst damage spikes.

Unlock: Available from start

Projectile Speed Tome

Projectile Speed | +15%

Increases the velocity of projectiles, improving hit accuracy and range.

Unlock: Available from start

Regen Tome

HP Regen | +40/min

Grants passive health regeneration per second, enabling sustained combat.

Unlock: Available from start

Shield Tome

Shield | +25

Increases shield capacity, providing a regenerating damage buffer.

Unlock: Available from start

Silver Tome

Silver Gain | +12%

Increases silver currency gain for meta progression purchases.

Unlock: Available from start

Size Tome

Attack Size | +10%

Increases the size of attacks and projectiles, improving screen coverage.

Unlock: Available from start

Unlockable Tomes

Complete specific challenges to unlock these powerful Tomes for future runs!

Armor Tome

Armor | +12%

Increases Armor, reducing incoming damage with diminishing returns at high values.

Unlock: Kill 5,000 enemies as Sir Oofie

Attraction Tome

Pickup Range | +75%

Increases the range at which you automatically collect XP and gold drops.

Unlock: Use Shrine of Succ 8 times

Bloody Tome

Lifesteal | +10%

Converts a percentage of damage dealt into health, providing sustain.

Unlock: Kill 12,500 enemies

Chaos Tome

1 Random Stat per Level | Max Lv. 99

Adds one randomly selected stat bonus per upgrade. Silver Gain and Jump Height are no longer in the pool.

Unlock: Charge every Charge Shrine across all 3 stages of one Tier 3 run without leaving the charge zone

Cursed Tome

Difficulty | +3.5%

Raises enemy quantity, health, speed and damage; the extra density also creates more XP and drops.

Unlock: Beat a Stage Boss in under 5 minutes

Duration Tome

Duration | +15%

Extends the duration of projectiles and effects, improving screen coverage.

Unlock: Upgrade Axe to Level 10

Luck Tome

Luck | +7%

Improves the quality of level-up offers, shrine rewards, and chest drops.

Unlock: 0.01% chance drop from any enemy

Quantity Tome

Projectile Count | +1

Adds extra projectiles to compatible weapons, multiplying damage output.

Unlock: Fire 5,000 projectiles

Thorns Tome

Retaliation | +15

Damages enemies when they hit you, rewarding tanky defensive builds.

Unlock: Block 250 attacks with Aegis

XP Tome

Experience Gain | +7%

Accelerates leveling speed, enabling faster build scaling and snowball.

Unlock: Break 20 pots in a single run
